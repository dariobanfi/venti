Most of us fly with five apps open: one for the forecast, one for the wind at the stations, one as a flight computer, one to post the flight, and a chat group to agree on where to go. None of them talk to each other. Venti puts those jobs in one place, so that a day is planned, flown, looked back on and shared in the same app.

It starts in the Alps. Today you can see what the forecast says about the take-offs around you, the wind measured right now, everyone's flights and the season's ranking. The phone apps carry a cockpit and a logbook, and the same flight shows up here a moment after you land. Next we bring the assistant into the air: ask it, by voice, how far the next thermal is or whether the landing is still in reach. The warnings that keep you safe, airspace and terrain, stay plain code that works without a network and without any AI.

## What we want it to be

- **Free, and open source.** Anyone can read the code, run it and make it better.
- **No advertising, no tracking.** Venti earns nothing and sells nothing.
- **Open data.** What pilots share, flights and take-offs and what they know of them, belongs to the community and can be read by anyone through Venti's API. Only the flights you keep private stay private.
- **AI native.** Everything Venti knows, the wind, the forecast, the sites, the airspace and your own flights, is there for your own assistant through MCP, the open standard Claude and Gemini speak. Ask it where to fly on Saturday, or what your last ten flights say about your climbing. Venti pays for no AI and keeps none of it: you bring your own.
- **Open to every country.** If your valley has a good source of wind or forecast, it can be plugged in, by a guide anyone can follow.

Venti is made by pilots, for pilots. Share a flight, add a take-off you know, tell us what is missing. Good flights, and see you in the air.

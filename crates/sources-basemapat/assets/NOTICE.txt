austria.geojson in this folder is Austria's outline, made from data of the
Bundesamt für Eich- und Vermessungswesen (BEV), "Verwaltungsgrenzen (VGD) -
Stichtagsdaten 1:50 000", release VGD_Oesterreich_gen_50_20221002.zip (key
date 2022-10-01), under the Creative Commons licence CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. It is not covered by the
licence of the code around it.

Credit, in our own words until BEV's own wording is found (to verify):
"Datenquelle: Bundesamt für Eich- und Vermessungswesen (BEV),
Verwaltungsgrenzen 1:50 000, Stichtag 01.10.2022, CC BY 4.0;
zusammengefasst, nach WGS 84 umgerechnet und vereinfacht".

What was done to it: the 7,850 cadastral municipalities were joined into
one outline, taken from MGI / Austria Lambert (EPSG:31287) to WGS 84 with
PROJ's "MGI to WGS 84 (8)", simplified by Douglas-Peucker at 5 m in the
metric projection and rounded to six decimals. Two parts, the main body
and Jungholz, which touches it at one point; no holes; 20,163 positions;
437,509 bytes. ../tools/build_outline.py makes it again. The licence was
read on 2026-11-20 from the data.gv.at catalogue record,
https://www.data.gv.at/katalog/dataset/6d731e6e-7dcf-4bde-8061-d0e195f62f6c

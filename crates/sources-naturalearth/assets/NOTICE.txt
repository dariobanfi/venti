europe.geojson in this folder holds the outlines of the countries of
Europe, made from Natural Earth's "Admin 0 - Countries" at 1:10m, version
5.1.1 (ne_10m_admin_0_countries.zip from naciscdn.org, downloaded
2026-11-23), which Natural Earth puts in the public domain: "All versions
of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in
the public domain." (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/,
read 2026-11-23). No credit is asked; "Made with Natural Earth" is the
line it offers.

What was done to it: the countries Natural Earth puts in Europe, and
Cyprus, kept, each by its ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code (ISO_A2_EH), clipped to
32° W to 45° E and 34° N to 72° N, each ring simplified by 0.001°
(Douglas-Peucker) and rounded to five decimals, an island of less than
about a square kilometre left out. ../tools/build_countries.py makes it
again.
